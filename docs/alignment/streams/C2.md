# Stream C2 (verbatim, section 6 of the orchestration brief)

Acceptance criteria below are condensed from Jira's Gherkin. Name tests after the scenarios.
Keys: HU-xx are stories; SCRUM-nn are subtasks. "Jira says" is the declared status; the work is to make the code match or exceed it.

### Stream C2: HU-06 Outfit recommendation (SCRUM-43, Jira En revisión) and HU-11 Feedback learning (SCRUM-74, Jira En curso)
HU-06 acceptance: the engine proposes the plan's number of garments (3 Esencial, 5 Estilo), all available in the subscriber's size, total cost not above the plan target cost; garments already received are not repeated; with no rating history, initial style rules from the quiz apply.
- SCRUM-44 initial style rules in `backend/data/seed/style-rules.json`, flagged "pendiente de validación del estilista", read by the engine.
- SCRUM-45 hard filters: variant available in the subscriber's size, stock available, budget and `targetCost`, line, item count per plan, no repetition (relax only if the catalog is exhausted and flag it in the explanation), cold-start rules.
- SCRUM-46 outfit composition: category slots plus a compatibility table in `seed/outfit-rules.json`; score the box as a whole. Remove the hardcoded outfit copy in `boxView.js`; render from persisted data.
- SCRUM-47 monthly batch job: for ACTIVE subscriptions with a confirmed charge and no box in the cycle, create a PENDING_REVIEW box; idempotent per subscription and cycle. Stop creating boxes on profile save.
- SCRUM-48 persist an explanation per item (matched attributes, score components, filters applied, model version) and show it to the stylist.
- SCRUM-49 offline evaluation: `scripts/eval-recommender.js`, `tests/fixtures/synthetic-profiles.json` (at least 30 profiles, varied sizes, plans, budgets, cold and warm), `npm run eval`. A test asserts 100% of hard constraints are satisfied; report style-match and diversity metrics.
HU-11 acceptance: after a subscriber rates garments low on style, the next box reduces the weight of similar attributes; stylist replacements are included as training examples when the model updates. (Wave 3, after ratings from E1.)
- SCRUM-75 ingestion job normalizing ratings and `training_examples` into `feedback_events`.
- SCRUM-76 real per-user `profile.weights` (bounded update rule, clamped 0..1, low style rating lowers the garment's attributes, high raises, replacements adjust), consumed by the engine. Remove the old "prepend style" heuristic.
- SCRUM-77 evaluation: average rating before versus after per user cohort (`scripts/eval-feedback.js` plus an admin endpoint).
- SCRUM-78 versioning: `model_versions` (id, ruleset hash, createdAt, active flag); every box stores its `modelVersion`; switching the active version is a config action.
