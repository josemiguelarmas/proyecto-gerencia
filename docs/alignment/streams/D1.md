# Stream D1 (verbatim, section 6 of the orchestration brief)

Acceptance criteria below are condensed from Jira's Gherkin. Name tests after the scenarios.
Keys: HU-xx are stories; SCRUM-nn are subtasks. "Jira says" is the declared status; the work is to make the code match or exceed it.

### Stream D1: HU-08 Plan and recurring payment, HU-12 Pause/change/cancel, HU-13 Exit survey
HU-08 (SCRUM-56, Jira Por hacer, starts tomorrow). Acceptance: choose Esencial or Estilo and register a valid payment method, first charge succeeds, subscription ACTIVE, confirmation email; rejected payment shows the reason and the subscription does not activate; a failed monthly charge is retried according to the policy and the user is notified to update the payment method.
- SCRUM-58 `subscriptions` model and state machine (F8), `PLAN_CONFIG`, billing cycle (`cycleAnchor`, `nextBillingAt`), stores only a payment token plus brand and last4.
- SCRUM-57 payment adapter with tokenization: the client sends a provider token, never card data. Reject (400) any payload with card-number-like fields (13 to 19 digits, `cardNumber`, `cvv`) and never log bodies. Frontend plan selection and payment screens use the fake provider flow. Document the real gateway steps in `docs/integrations.md`.
- SCRUM-59 `POST /api/payments/webhook` with HMAC signature verification, idempotency by event id; reconciliation job comparing local charges to provider events.
- SCRUM-60 retry policy from config and notification emails; after the last retry the subscription becomes PAST_DUE.
- SCRUM-61 commission 3.5% per charge stored in integer COP, plus an admin report endpoint.
- SCRUM-62 sandbox tests: success, declined, recurring failure then retries then PAST_DUE, duplicate webhook, bad signature, unverified email blocked.
HU-12 (SCRUM-79, Jira En curso). Acceptance: pausing for 1 month means no charge and no box that month and automatic resume afterwards; changing from Esencial to Estilo before the cutoff date applies from the next box; cancelling stops future charges and offers pause as an alternative before confirming.
- SCRUM-80 transitions through the state machine with guards (served backend, not localStorage).
- SCRUM-81 cutoff date per cycle from config; after the cutoff the change applies the following cycle and the UI says so.
- SCRUM-82 "Mi suscripción" view routed in `frontend/js/app.js` (port from `js/screens/plan.js`).
- SCRUM-83 retention flow: cancel first returns a pause offer; cancellation completes only on explicit confirmation.
- SCRUM-84 tests for every valid and invalid transition, including the auto-resume job.
HU-13 (SCRUM-85, Jira Finalizado). Acceptance: after confirming cancellation the user picks a reason from a list and optionally a comment, saved against the cancellation.
- SCRUM-86 reasons in config: precio, estilo no acertó, talla, tiempo, otro.
- SCRUM-87 optional, non-blocking form: cancellation succeeds even if the survey is skipped; `exit_surveys` collection linked to the cancellation.
- SCRUM-88 aggregated report endpoint and admin view, consumed later by HU-17.
